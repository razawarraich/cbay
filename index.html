Questions

What is an apostille?

An apostille is a certificate from the Secretary of State that makes a document valid in countries that joined the Hague Convention.

Do I need to go to Sacramento?

No. We make the trip for you and return the finished document.

How fast can I get it back?

24-hour turnaround is available. Call to confirm timing for your documents.

Do you offer notary service?

Yes. We are California Bay Mobile Apostille & Notary, so we can notarize documents that need it first.
